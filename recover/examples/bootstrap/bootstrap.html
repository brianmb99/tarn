    Technical details
      What is this page?

      __APP_NAME__ stores your data, encrypted, on Arweave — a permanent storage network. Recovery pages (which decrypt your data entirely in your browser) are published to Arweave too, but Arweave content can never be edited, so each improved recovery page lives at a new address. This small page is the stable front door: it finds the newest recovery page published by the app's operator and forwards you to it.

      Lookups are pinned to the operator's signing key, so only pages the operator actually published can ever be offered here. This page takes no input and asks for no credentials.
