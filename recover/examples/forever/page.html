Recover your data

This page reads your data directly from Arweave and decrypts it in your browser. Tarn's servers are not contacted at any point. Nothing you type leaves this tab.

What is this page?

Tarn-backed apps store your data, encrypted, on Arweave — a permanent storage network. The keys that decrypt your data are derived from your credentials. This page exercises that property directly: given your account key (or username + password), it downloads your encrypted data from a public Arweave gateway and decrypts it locally.

You can use this page even if the app you used is offline, even if Tarn's servers are down, even if the company is gone. As long as Arweave is online and you remember your credentials (or kept your account key), your data is recoverable.

This page is open-source and reproducible. If you have the page file saved locally, you don't even need this site to be online.

1. Which app's data?

The appId is the identifier the app used when you registered. Apps that ship a themed copy of this page pin it automatically.

Schema

Paste the app's schema JSON below. The app developer publishes this — it tells the recovery tool which collections you have and how to interpret each record's fields.

2. How do you want to sign in?

Your account key is the 24-word phrase you saved when the app first asked you to back up your account. It is the durable, password-independent way to recover. Paste it below — words separated by single spaces.

Use your username and the password you remember. (Tarn doesn't have a password reset — if you've forgotten the password and don't have your account key, the data is unrecoverable. That's the cost of zero-knowledge encryption.)

3. Arweave gateways

Arweave is a network of independent gateways. We try them in order; if one is down, we move to the next. The defaults are fine. Override only if you know a specific gateway is healthier right now.
